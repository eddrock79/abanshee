Taps, showers and outlets one per line: name | hot, cold or mixed | little used (yes/no)

Little-used outlets get flushed weekly. Hot water should reach at least 50°C within a minute (60°C stored in the tank); cold should be below 20°C within 2 minutes.

Fire risk assessment

Required by the Fire (Scotland) Act 2005. Work through each section, record the significant findings, and add an action for anything that needs doing. Review it at least once a year, and whenever something changes (layout, events, number of people).

The fire risk assessment is edited by managers. Log in as a manager to change it.
